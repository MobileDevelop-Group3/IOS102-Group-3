### Member 1: Amanda Torres

1. **Abuzz; Dating app**
* **Category:** Photo/Video, Social
* **Description:** Allows users to hone in on whoever makes them buzz! Lots of bee puns throughout the app. Users can specify what makes them buzz vs what makes them sting.


2. **Novelity; Goodreads clone**
* **Category:** Social Cataloging/ Book Centric
* **Description:** Users can bookmark and categorize their book reads. They may provide rankings for their top reads, write comments, and give ratings.


3. **Pinstart; Pinterest clone**
* **Category:** Social Cataloging/Discovery, Visuals
* **Description:** User can create a visual board for an occasion or theme, by uploading images from their device or adding any of the suggested.

### Member 2: Jherson Hernandez

1. **Trip Mate: Travel Planner**
* **Category:** Travel, Productivity
* **Description:** Users can create trips, save destinations, organize activities by day, view locations on a map, and share itineraries with friends.


2. **RepTrack: Fitness tracker**
* **Category:** Health & Fitness, Productivity
* **Description:** Users can create workout routines, track exercises, record weights and repetitions, and monitor their progress over time.


3. **LinkUp: Social event discovery**
* **Category:** Social, Entertainment
* **Description:** Users can discover nearby events, create plans with friends, RSVP to activities, and share events they are interested in.
