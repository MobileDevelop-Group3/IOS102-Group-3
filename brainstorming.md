## Top Ideas - Evaluate and Categorize

### 1. CampusSpot - Campus Study Space Finder

* **Description:** CampusSpot is a mobile application designed for university students to discover, review, and bookmark the best study spots on campus. Whether you're looking for a quiet corner, a collaborative group room, or a hidden gem on campus; CampusSpot helps you find your ideal productivity environment based on real student ratings and live filters.

* **Category:** Education / Productivity / Utility

* **Mobile:** Designed primarily as a mobile application (iOS) for students on the move, with responsive layouts for quick on-campus access.

* **Story:** Students often struggle to find a suitable place to study on campus, especially during busy periods such as midterms and finals. They may spend time walking between buildings looking for available seating, quiet areas, or places with power outlets. CampusSpot solves this problem by allowing students to browse locations, compare ratings and reviews, apply filters, and save their favorite study spots.

* **Market:** College and university students, study groups, and campus organizations.
  
* **Habit:** Daily or weekly use app, especially utilized during midterms, finals, or between classes when students need a reliable workspace.
  
* **Scope:**
  * **V1:** User registration and login, home feed displaying study locations, search and filters, location detail pages with ratings and reviews, submitting reviews with photo uploads, and saving favorite locations to a Saved Spots list.
  * **V2:** Interactive campus map, improved location browsing, additional filtering options, and a user profile displaying review and bookmark statistics.
  * **V3:** Crowdsourced busyness indicators, improved location availability information, and additional community features such as editing or deleting personal reviews and photos.


### 2. Trip Mate - Travel Planner

* **Description:** Trip Mate is a mobile travel planning application that allows users to organize trips, save destinations, and create daily itineraries. Users can add activities, organize places they want to visit, and keep their travel plans in one convenient location.

* **Category:** Travel / Lifestyle / Productivity

* **Mobile:** Trip Mate uses mobile features such as GPS, interactive maps, location services, and push notifications. Users can view nearby attractions, navigate between destinations, and receive reminders about planned activities. The camera could also allow users to capture and save travel memories within their trips.

* **Story:** Planning a trip often involves using multiple applications, websites, and notes to organize destinations, reservations, and activities. Trip Mate provides one place where travelers can organize their plans and keep track of their daily activities.

* **Market:** The target audience includes travelers, college students, families, and groups of friends who plan domestic or international trips. The market is broad because people travel for vacations, school breaks, business, and personal experiences.

* **Habit:** Users would primarily open the app when preparing for a trip and throughout their travels. Usage could increase significantly during vacations as users check their itineraries, navigate destinations, and update their plans. Users also create content by adding destinations, activities, and personal notes.

* **Scope:**
  * **V1:** User accounts, create and manage trips, add destinations, organize activities by day, and view trip itineraries.
  * **V2:** Map integration, location-based recommendations, trip sharing, and collaboration with other travelers.
  * **V3:** Smart itinerary suggestions, travel notifications, weather integration, and personalized destination recommendations.


### 3. SplitWise - Shared Expense Tracker

* **Description:** SplitWise is a mobile application that helps groups of people track and divide shared expenses. Users can create groups, add expenses, assign costs to group members, and view who owes money. The app simplifies expense management for roommates, friends, families, and people traveling together.

* **Category:** Finance / Productivity / Utility

* **Mobile:** SplitWise uses mobile features such as push notifications, camera access, and real-time updates. Users can take pictures of receipts, add expenses while making purchases, and receive notifications when new expenses are added or balances are updated.

* **Story:** When people share expenses, keeping track of who paid and how much everyone owes can become confusing. This often leads to disagreements or forgotten payments. SplitWise addresses this problem by organizing shared expenses and automatically calculating how much each person owes.

* **Market:** The target audience includes college students, roommates, families, friends, and travelers who regularly share expenses. The potential market is broad because shared spending happens in many everyday situations, from splitting rent and groceries to managing vacation costs.

* **Habit:** Users could open the app whenever they make a shared purchase, add an expense, or check their group balances. Usage may be especially frequent among roommates and groups that regularly share costs. Users actively contribute by entering expenses, uploading receipts, and updating payment information.

* **Scope:**
  * **V1:** User registration and login, create expense groups, add shared expenses, assign expenses to members, and calculate individual balances.
  * **V2:** Receipt photo uploads, expense categories, payment tracking, and push notifications.
  * **V3:** Payment service integration, spending analytics, recurring expenses, and advanced group expense management.
